Frequently asked questions.

What technologies do you use?

We build with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Our sites are deployed on Vercel for global edge performance. For CMS needs we integrate Sanity, Contentful, or headless WordPress.

Do you offer SEO?

Every website we build includes technical SEO foundations: structured data, semantic HTML, Core Web Vitals optimization, meta tags, and XML sitemaps. We also offer ongoing SEO strategy as an add-on service.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the pages, features, content and languages you need. Fill in the project brief to see a live estimate as you answer, and after a short call we send a fixed quote so there are no surprises.
