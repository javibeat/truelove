The Story

We come from the music world. Before designing for artists, we were one.

Javi Beat — founder of TRUE LOVE CREATIVE — has been part of the Dubai music scene for over a decade. As a DJ, he understands what artists need: not just a website, but a complete visual identity that translates the energy of your sound into design.

That's why the artists we work with don't get generic templates. They get brands built by someone who knows the industry from the inside.

WHAT WE BUILD FOR ARTISTS

Artist Websites & EPK

Your digital home. Bio, music, tour dates, press kit — everything a booker or fan needs, in one place.

Brand Identity & Logo

A logo and visual system that captures your sound. From typography to color palette — your brand, defined.

Social Media Kits

Templates for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube. Consistent visuals that make every post look intentional.

Press Kits & Promo

Professional press materials that get you booked. One-sheets, rider templates, and promotional decks.

Album & Single Artwork

Cover art that stops the scroll. Designed for Spotify, Beatport, vinyl — every format, every platform.
